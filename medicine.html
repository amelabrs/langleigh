Good to have

Emergency: 112. Ambulance: 108. Check expiry dates every 6 months. Ticks are saved on this device only.

Which one for...

  • Fever: Paracetamol
  • Headache or toothache: Crocin Pain Relief, Dart, Paracetamol, Ibuprofen or Hifenac-P
  • Sprain or sore muscle: Ibuprofen, Crepe bandage
  • Loose motions: ORS first, Imodium if needed
  • Heartburn or acidity: Antacid chewable
  • Sore throat: Strepsils
  • Itchy bite, heat rash, sunburn: Calamine lotion or Lactocalamine
  • Cut or scrape: Neomycin, then a band-aid
  • Ringworm or fungal rash: Clotrimazole
  • Sneezing or allergic itch: Allegra

Do not double up

  • Paracetamol group: Paracetamol, Crocin Pain Relief, Hifenac-P and Dart all contain paracetamol. Count them together toward the daily limit.
  • Painkiller (NSAID) group: Ibuprofen, Nucoxia and Hifenac-P (aceclofenac) are all NSAIDs. Do not take them together.
